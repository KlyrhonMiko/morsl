# Visual assets

Quicksand and Caveat are bundled from the Google Fonts repository. Their SIL Open
Font License files are included beside the fonts in `assets/fonts/`.

Sample photos are downloaded from Unsplash and used only for the removable,
local example scrapbook and its welcome image. No network images are required
to browse the installed examples.

- salad.jpg: https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1547592180-85f173990554
- pasta.jpg: https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1473093295043-cdd812d0e601
- pizza.jpg: https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1565299624946-b28f40a0ae38
- pastry.jpg: https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1555507036-ab1f4038808a

All controls use Flutter's Material vector icon family. The small paper texture
and illustrative map are drawn by the app. They are not geographic data.
